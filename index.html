A clean path from deal to decision

Role-based workflows keep dealer staff, underwriters and finance on their own lanes.

Create the deal

Dealer staff enter vehicle and subscriber (NIN + phone) details and submit for underwriting.

Capture consent

A one-time OTP to the subscriber's phone records immutable consent — nothing is scored without it.

Assess risk

The system pulls the gnuGrid mobile score plus an internal asset score into a combined tier and recommended structure.

Decide & activate

Underwriters approve or decline; finance initiates MTN/Airtel Request-to-Pay and activates the subscription.
